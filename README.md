## 👋 About Me

Hey, I'm Satya Sai Ganesh Yerramsetti — an AI/ML engineer who genuinely enjoys turning complex research into systems that actually work in the real world.

I am a recent Master's graduate in Computer Science at the **University of North Texas**, where I have spent most of my time into building things I find genuinely interesting: autonomous agents that think through problems, LLM pipelines that scale, and deep learning systems for medical imaging.

Before that I spent over a year as an **Applied Scientist intern at CDK Global**, working on ML systems for one of the largest automotive retail platforms in the US. That experience taught me a lot about what it means to build ML that works under real constraints — not just on a notebook.

A few things that define how I work:
- I default to building things end-to-end rather than stopping at a proof of concept
- I care about making AI systems explainable and observable, not just accurate
- I believe the best engineering solutions are the ones that are actually used

When I'm not coding, I'm usually playing cricket, hitting the gym, or sketching something out on paper.
